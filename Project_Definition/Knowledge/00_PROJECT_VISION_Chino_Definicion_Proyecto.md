Why does this project exist?
==========================================

==========================================
PROJECT
==========================================

Knowledge Management System supporting the Bachelor's Degree in Asian and African Studies (Chinese Track) at the Autonomous University of Madrid.

Planned duration: 2026 – 2030

==========================================
MISSION
==========================================

To build an integrated learning, research and knowledge management system that enables a deep multidisciplinary understanding of China by combining Mandarin Chinese language acquisition with the study of Chinese history, geography, society, economy, politics, culture, science and technology.

The project is not intended solely to complete a university degree.

Its objective is to build a structured, verifiable, reusable and continuously evolving Knowledge Base that will remain valuable beyond the completion of the official academic programme.

==========================================
VISION
==========================================

At the completion of the project, the repository should be capable of:

- documenting the complete learning journey;
- providing rapid access to any previously studied concept;
- connecting knowledge across different academic subjects;
- supporting future research activities;
- facilitating the development of the Bachelor's Thesis (TFG);
- supporting lifelong learning beyond university.

==========================================
SCOPE
==========================================

The project includes:
- Mandarin Chinese language acquisition;
- Chinese history;
- physical and human geography;
- domestic and international politics;
- economics;
- society;
- philosophy and intellectual traditions;
- literature;
- art;
- science;
- technology;
- engineering;
- megaprojects;
- mountaineering;
- business culture;
- China–Spain relations;
- observation of the Chinese community in Spain;
- preparation for an academic stay in China;
- iterative development of the Bachelor's Thesis (TFG).

The governance framework shall not merely organize documents.

It shall support the Knowledge Lifecycle defined in 01_KNOWLEDGE_MATURITY.md, ensuring that knowledge evolves in a traceable, reusable and continuously improvable manner.


==========================================
OUT OF SCOPE
==========================================

This project does not aim to:

- replace university education;
- replace primary academic sources;
- generate unsupported knowledge;
- store redundant information;
- prioritize memorization over understanding;
- pursue academic certifications as the sole objective.

==========================================
FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES
==========================================

01. Truthfulness
Every significant statement shall be supported by evidence or explicitly identified as a hypothesis.
Information shall never be invented.

------------------------------------------
02. Critical Thinking
Conclusions shall remain open to continuous review.
AI assistants shall not automatically validate user assumptions.


------------------------------------------
03. Academic Rigor
Whenever reasonably possible, primary sources, official documentation and academic literature shall be preferred.


------------------------------------------
04. Interdisciplinary Learning
Knowledge shall be organized through relationships between disciplines rather than isolated academic subjects.


------------------------------------------
05. Continuous Evolution
All knowledge shall be able to evolve without losing traceability.


------------------------------------------
06. Reusability
Every knowledge asset should remain reusable for:
- research;
- examination preparation;
- Bachelor's Thesis (TFG);
- future learning.

------------------------------------------
07. Evidence Before Opinion
Important decisions shall be supported by identifiable evidence.
Insufficient evidence shall always be explicitly indicated.

------------------------------------------
08. Understanding Before Memorization
Understanding shall always have priority over remembering isolated facts.

------------------------------------------
09. Quality Before Quantity
A smaller amount of well-structured knowledge is preferred over large volumes of disorganized information.

------------------------------------------
10. Continuous Improvement
The system shall be periodically reviewed in order to identify:
- inconsistencies;
- duplication;
- obsolete knowledge;
- opportunities for improvement.

==========================================
ROLE OF AI
==========================================

AI assistants act as specialized collaborators whose purpose is to accelerate
learning and improve the quality of the project's knowledge base.

Depending on the task, AI may operate as:
- tutor;
- critical reviewer;
- researcher;
- translator;
- knowledge architect;
- methodological assistant;
- consistency supervisor.
AI shall not replace the student's own reasoning nor constitute an academic authority.

==========================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
==========================================

The project shall be considered successful if, upon completion of the degree, it provides:
- functional proficiency in Mandarin Chinese;
- a coherent and comprehensive knowledge repository;
- an evidence-based Bachelor's Thesis (TFG);
- a reusable methodology for future studies;
- the capability for autonomous lifelong learning.

Success shall not be measured solely through academic grades.

==========================================
DOCUMENT AUTHORITY
==========================================

This document defines the strategic vision of the project.

All architectural documents, governance documents, protocols, methodologies and operational procedures shall remain consistent with the principles defined here.

Modifications to this document shall be exceptional and justified only by significant strategic changes affecting the project.
