# PURPOSE

This document defines what learning means within the project.

Its objective is to describe how knowledge is acquired, integrated, retained and applied throughout the academic journey.

This document models learning.

It does NOT prescribe specific study techniques or software tools.

==========================================
RESPONSIBILITIES
==========================================

This document defines:
- learning stages;
- learning objectives;
- competency progression;
- knowledge integration;
- learning validation;
- AI responsibilities.

It does NOT define:
- study schedules;
- exams;
- flashcards;
- learning resources;
- AI governance.

==========================================
FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES
==========================================

Learning is a continuous process.
Understanding has priority over memorization.
Application has priority over repetition.
Knowledge becomes meaningful through relationships.
Learning shall remain cumulative.
Knowledge may require periodic reinforcement.
Learning objectives depend on the nature of the knowledge.

==========================================
LEARNING STAGES
==========================================

Stage 0
Exposure

The learner becomes aware of the concept.

------------------------------------------
Stage 1
Recognition

The learner recognizes the concept.

------------------------------------------
Stage 2
Understanding

The learner understands its meaning.

------------------------------------------
Stage 3
Application

The learner can use the knowledge appropriately.

------------------------------------------
Stage 4
Integration

The learner relates the knowledge to other concepts.

------------------------------------------
Stage 5
Mastery

The learner can explain, compare, evaluate and teach the concept.

==========================================
LEARNING VALIDATION
==========================================

Learning should be demonstrated through application.

Possible indicators include:
correct explanation;
correct use;
problem solving;
translation;
comparison;
teaching another person;
integration with previous knowledge.


==========================================
LEARNING EVOLUTION
==========================================

Learning is dynamic.

Knowledge may:

grow;
be reinforced;
be refined;
be forgotten;
be rediscovered.

The project shall support continuous improvement rather than binary success or failure.


==========================================
SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
==========================================

Language acquisition requires active use.
Chinese characters require independent reinforcement.
Engineering knowledge benefits from visual and geographical context.
Field observations strengthen conceptual understanding.
Mountaineering, travel and direct cultural experience are considered valuable
learning contexts when they reinforce academic knowledge.

==========================================
AI RESPONSIBILITIES
==========================================

AI assistants shall:

adapt explanations to the learner's stage;
promote understanding before memorization;
encourage knowledge integration;
identify conceptual gaps;
suggest reinforcement when appropriate;
avoid unnecessary repetition;
recommend progressively more challenging tasks.

==========================================
ARCHITECTURAL CONSTRAINTS
==========================================

The learning model shall remain independent from:

specific pedagogical methodologies;
software platforms;
AI systems;
assessment methods;
future educational technologies.

==========================================
FUNDAMENTAL RULE
==========================================

Learning is considered successful when knowledge becomes understandable, usable, interconnected and reusable rather than merely remembered.
